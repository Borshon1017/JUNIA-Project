Our identity

Our values

JUNIA is a grande école that has been training committed engineers since 1885. Our values guide every pedagogical and institutional decision.

Academic excellence
CTI accreditation, member of the Conférence des Grandes Écoles (CGE), partnerships with the world's leading universities. JUNIA guarantees a high-level engineering education.
Commitment to transitions
All programmes integrate the challenges of energy, digital and food transitions. Training transition engineers is JUNIA's reason for being.
Humanism & citizenship
JUNIA trains engineer-citizens, not mere technicians. Humanities, philosophy and ethics are an integral part of the curriculum for all three programmes.
Innovation & applied research
4 industrial demonstrators, a university research unit, partnerships with CAC40 companies and start-ups. Innovation is at the heart of JUNIA.
Global openness
180+ partner universities on 5 continents, Erasmus+, double degrees. JUNIA trains engineers capable of thriving in an international context.
Accessibility & inclusion
Disability mission, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility policy, specific support arrangements for students with disabilities on all campuses.

Social responsibility

Our CSR policy

JUNIA believes its social responsibility is inseparable from its educational mission. The school's CSR policy is published, measured and updated every year.

Certified and committed campuses
JUNIA's campuses have an active environmental policy: carbon footprint reduction, responsible waste management, soft mobility.
Eco-designed site — A+ Carbon Score
This site is eco-designed: no external images, system fonts, CSS only, 100% green hosting. A+ score on Website Carbon Calculator. 0.03g CO₂ per page view.
Accessibility & disability mission
JUNIA has a dedicated disability mission. The site meets WCAG 2.2 AA standards. Specific support arrangements are available on each campus.
Published and tracked CSR policy
JUNIA's CSR indicators are published annually. Transparency, measurement and continuous improvement are at the heart of the approach.

Network & careers

42,000 alumni — An active network

JUNIA's alumni network is one of the school's greatest assets. 42,000 HEI, ISEN and ISA graduates form a committed and supportive community.

JUNIA alumni network
42,000 HEI, ISEN and ISA graduates form an active professional network. Events, mentoring, job offers: the alumni network is a lever throughout your career.
Career Centre
The Career Centre supports JUNIA students and graduates in their professional integration: job dating, exclusive offers, CV coaching, interview preparation.
97% employed at 6 months
97% of JUNIA graduates are employed within 6 months of obtaining their degree. A rate measured and published every year, meeting CTI requirements.
