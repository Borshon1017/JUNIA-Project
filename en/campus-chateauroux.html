The city & region

Studying in Châteauroux

Châteauroux and the Indre department offer a peaceful, green living environment suited to studying and personal growth, in a region with a strong industrial and agricultural base.

Living environment
A calm and green setting ideal for focused study. Châteauroux offers a more peaceful lifestyle than major cities — perfect for students seeking a human-scale campus with close-knit cohorts.
Regional industry
The Centre-Val de Loire region has a diverse industrial base: manufacturing, agri-food, defence and aeronautics. A rich pool of opportunities for internships and work-study placements.
Human-scale campus
A campus where every student is known and personally supported. Closer ties between students and teaching staff encourage individualised guidance and a strong sense of cohort community.
Health services
A health service is available on campus: GP, nurse and psychological support. JUNIA looks after the physical and mental well-being of its students on all its sites.
