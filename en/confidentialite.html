Privacy Policy

Data controller

The data controller for personal data collected on this website is JUNIA — Grande École des Transitions, 2 rue Norbert Segard, 59000 Lille, France.

Data collected

JUNIA collects personal data only when you voluntarily provide it (contact form, brochure request, admissions application). The data collected may include: last name, first name, email address, telephone number and your enquiry.

Purpose of processing

Your data is used exclusively to respond to your enquiry, process your application or send you the requested document. It is never sold or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes.

Your rights

In accordance with GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, delete and port your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact JUNIA's data protection officer at the address shown on the Contact page.

Data retention

Data from contact requests is kept for a maximum of 3 years from the last contact. Application data is kept in accordance with applicable regulations on educational records.

Cookies

This website uses only strictly necessary technical cookies. No advertising, tracking or social media cookies are used.
