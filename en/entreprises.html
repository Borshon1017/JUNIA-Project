Partnership

Why become a JUNIA partner?

By partnering with JUNIA, you gain access to an innovation ecosystem, a network of 42,000 alumni, and programmes directly anchored in industry needs.

Recruit talent trained for transitions
JUNIA engineers are trained for energy, digital and food transitions. They are operational and motivated from day one in the company.
Offer work-study contracts
Work-study at JUNIA lasts 3 years. It lets you train a future engineer directly in your professional environment, with the school's academic support.
Co-build research projects
JUNIA has 4 industrial demonstrators and a research unit. Applied research partnerships allow co-development of innovative solutions.
Apprenticeship levy & sponsorship
Direct your apprenticeship levy to JUNIA and benefit from a tax deduction. Sponsorship is also possible to support educational or research projects.
Rent our spaces
JUNIA campuses offer modular spaces, lecture theatres and seminar rooms available for hire for your professional events.
Employer brand & visibility
JUNIA company partners gain visibility among 5,000 students and 42,000 alumni: job fairs, presentations, forums, joint projects.
Innovation

Our industrial demonstrators

JUNIA has 4 unique demonstrators in France, open to industrial partners for collaborative research projects and on-site training.

Factory of the future
A demonstrator dedicated to Industry 4.0: collaborative robots, cobots, industrial IoT, digital twins. A real testing ground for student and industrial projects.
City in transition
This demonstrator explores the sustainable city of tomorrow: smart city, urban network management, sustainable mobility, smart building and urban energy transition.
Agriculture & food of tomorrow
This ISA demonstrator is dedicated to agroecology, sustainable food systems, agricultural transition and food innovation. A unique space connected to the field.

Research & Innovation

Our research themes

JUNIA's academic research is organised around the major transitions. Company partnerships are possible in each of these areas.

Accelerating energy & urban transition
Intelligent energy systems, smart grids, positive-energy buildings, sustainable cities. JUNIA conducts applied research in partnership with industry players.
Driving digital & industrial transition
Artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, embedded systems, automation. JUNIA's research contributes to the digital transformation of companies and industries.
Feeding the planet sustainably
Agroecology, sustainable food, plant biology, resource management. ISA research provides concrete solutions to food and environmental challenges.
Advancing health & well-being technologies
Connected health, biomedical, air and water quality, well-being. Interdisciplinary research at the crossroads of digital, energy and life sciences.
