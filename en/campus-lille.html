Lille Campus

JUNIA's historic campus since 1885. Located in the heart of greater Lille, it brings together four iconic sites — IC1, IC2, ALG and the Palais Rameaux — and hosts the HEI, ISEN and ISA programmes.

Facilities

The four sites of the Lille campus

JUNIA's Lille campus is organised around four complementary buildings, each dedicated to a specific dimension of education and school life.

IC1
IC1
Ingénieur Campus 1
The main teaching building, IC1 houses lecture theatres, classrooms and laboratories for the HEI and ISEN programmes. It also hosts the administrative offices, library and student services.

IC2 complements IC1 with spaces dedicated to practical work, workshops and student projects. It hosts the industrial demonstrators, co-working areas and cross-programme project rooms.

The city

Studying in Lille

Capital of Hauts-de-France, Lille is one of France's major student cities — a rich cultural scene, a dynamic city and an exceptional geographic position.

Location
Lille is 1h from Paris by high-speed train and 30 min from Brussels. At the crossroads of Northern Europe, the city offers easy access to major European capitals and their job markets.
Student city
With over 110,000 students, Lille is France's 4th largest university city. Vibrant cultural scene, thriving associations and a well-developed student accommodation offer.
Job market
The greater Lille area is one of France's top economic hubs. A strong presence of industrial groups, tech startups and companies in energy, digital and agri-food sectors.
Health services
A health service is available on campus: GP, nurse and psychological support. JUNIA looks after the physical and mental well-being of its students throughout their studies.
