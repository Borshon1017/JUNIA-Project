Our campuses

3 campuses to study in France

JUNIA is present on 3 campuses in France. Each campus provides a complete study environment with modern facilities and an active student life.

LIL
Main campus · Northern France
Lille Campus

Lille campus is JUNIA's historic and main campus. Located in the greater Lille area, it hosts all three HEI, ISEN and ISA programmes. Lille is a vibrant student city, capital of Hauts-de-France, 1h from Paris and 30 min from Brussels by train.

Bordeaux Campus

Bordeaux campus welcomes JUNIA students in the Bordeaux metropolitan area, renowned for its quality of life and economic dynamism. Bordeaux is an attractive city close to the Atlantic and Pyrenees, with a strong industrial and technology ecosystem.

Châteauroux Campus

The Châteauroux campus offers a calmer, human-scale study environment. Located in the Indre department, it is particularly suited to ISA programme students. A campus focused on nature, agricultural research and the environment.

Student services

Support for all students

JUNIA offers a comprehensive range of services to support students throughout their studies.

Accommodation
Student residences, private apartments, colocation: JUNIA helps you find accommodation on and around each campus. International students receive specific support.
Transport
Each campus is well connected to the city and region by public transport. Cycling facilities and car parks are also available on campus.
Health & well-being
A health service is available on each campus: GP, psychologist, nurse. JUNIA is committed to the physical and mental well-being of all students.
Student associations
More than 40 student associations on all campuses: sport, culture, humanitarian, entrepreneurship. A rich and diverse student life.
Disability mission
JUNIA has a dedicated disability mission. Specific arrangements are available for students with disabilities: facilities, academic adjustments, dedicated support.
Career Centre
The Career Centre accompanies students from year 1 in their professional project: CV workshops, interview preparation, job fairs, networking events.
