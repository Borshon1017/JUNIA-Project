Identity & Positioning

ISA, the life sciences engineer
in service of the planet

ISA — Institut Supérieur d'Agriculture — is JUNIA's Grande École programme dedicated to agricultural, food and environmental transitions. In a world confronted by the challenges of food security, climate change and depletion of natural resources, ISA trains engineers capable of proposing sustainable and innovative solutions.

ISA is distinguished by a rigorously scientific approach to agronomic sciences, combined with a systemic vision of food supply chains and a strong management focus. ISA engineers work across the entire agricultural and food value chain: from field to fork, from research to business management.

Agroecology & Life sciences
Plant and animal biology, genetics, microbiology, agroecosystem ecology.
Food science & Quality
Food technology, quality and food safety, product innovation, regulation.
Management & Sustainable development
Agricultural project management, economics, international development, CSR and reporting.

Specialities & Fields

ISA specialisation areas

From year 3, ISA students specialise according to their career goals: agronomy, food, environment or international development.

Agroecology & Sustainable agriculture
Agroecological production systems, conservation agriculture, permaculture, agroforestry, sustainable livestock, biodiversity and ecosystem services.
Food science & Technology
Food product formulation and innovation, transformation processes, preservation, sustainable packaging, sensory quality, nutrition and health.
Biotechnology & Applied biology
Genetic engineering, industrial fermentation, molecular biology applied to agriculture and food, bioinformatics, plant health and phytopathology.
Environment & Resource management
Water and soil management, environmental assessment, ecotoxicology, nature-based solutions, climate change adaptation.
International development & Agribusiness
Global agricultural markets, development projects in emerging countries, international cooperation, global food supply chains.
Agri-food supply chain management
Agri-food business management, food marketing, finance, sector CSR, quality management (ISO 22000, BRC, IFS).
