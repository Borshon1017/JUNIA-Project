Identity & Positioning

ISEN, the digital engineer
and intelligent systems specialist

ISEN — Ingénieurs en Systèmes Électroniques et Numériques — is JUNIA's Grande École programme dedicated to digital transitions. In a context of accelerated digital transformation, ISEN trains engineers capable of designing, developing and securing the digital systems of the future.

From artificial intelligence to cybersecurity, via embedded systems and advanced electronics, ISEN covers the entire digital ecosystem. The programme emphasises end-to-end mastery of systems: from hardware design to the most abstract software layers.

AI & Data specialisation
Machine learning, deep learning, massive data processing, computer vision and NLP.
Cybersecurity expertise
Systems security, cryptography, ethical hacking, GDPR compliance and ISO 27001 standards.
Embedded systems & IoT
Microcontrollers, FPGA, communication protocols, connected sensor networks.

Specialities & Fields

ISEN specialisation areas

From year 3, ISEN students specialise in the leading fields of digital and intelligent systems.

Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
Supervised and unsupervised machine learning, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, data pipelines.
Cybersecurity
Information systems security, penetration testing, digital forensics, applied cryptography, security governance (ISO 27001, ANSSI), SOC and SIEM.
Embedded Systems & IoT
Microprocessor and microcontroller architectures, real-time programming (RTOS), FPGA, IoT protocols (MQTT, LoRaWAN, Zigbee), industrial connected objects.
Networks & Telecommunications
Network architectures, TCP/IP protocols, 5G, SDN, network virtualisation (NFV), QoS management, carrier networks, cloud networking.
Cloud & DevOps
Microservices architectures, containers (Docker, Kubernetes), CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code (Terraform), cloud deployment (AWS, Azure, GCP).
Advanced Software Development
Software architecture, design patterns, software engineering, automated testing, backend and frontend development, RESTful and GraphQL APIs.
