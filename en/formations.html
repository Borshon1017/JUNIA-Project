Flagship programme

Grande École Engineering Cycle

5 years of study, CTI degree, integrated preparatory cycle. The royal road to the accredited engineering title.

Bac → Bac+5

Preparatory cycle + engineering cycle

The JUNIA Grande École engineering cycle lasts 5 years after school. The first two years are the integrated preparatory cycle, shared across all three HEI, ISEN and ISA programmes. The following three years focus on in-depth specialisation, where the student chooses their track.

CTI Accredited
Engineering degree recognised by the Commission des Titres d'Ingénieur.
Integrated preparatory cycle
No external prep school needed. The preparatory cycle is built into the first two years.
3 specialisations available
HEI (energy, buildings, industry), ISEN (digital, intelligent systems), ISA (agriculture, food, environment).

Work-based learning

Work-Study Engineer

Combine studies and professional experience. A CTI engineering degree while being paid.

Work-study rhythm
Alternating between company and school periods over 3 years (Bac+2 to Bac+5). Duration and rhythm adapted to the chosen programme.
Apprenticeship or professional contract
Apprenticeship contract or professionalisation contract. Training funded by the company, guaranteed remuneration.
CTI Engineering Degree
On completion of the 3 years, the CTI-accredited engineering degree is awarded — identical to the full-time programme.

All programmes

All pathways into JUNIA

JUNIA offers several levels and modes of study to suit every situation.

Masters & Specialised Degrees (Bac+5)
Masters in partnership with world universities and CGE-labelled Specialised Masters. Accessible from Bac+4. Duration: 1 to 2 years.
Bachelor in Software Development (Bac+3)
3-year programme after school. Fast entry into the software development world. Available on Parcoursup.
Continuing Education & RPL
Flexible programmes for working professionals. CPF-eligible. Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) available.
Staggered intake
Mid-year entry for the engineering preparatory cycle. An additional opportunity to join JUNIA outside the standard calendar.
International Double Degrees
180+ partner universities on 5 continents. Erasmus+ programmes. Spend 1 year abroad and return with two recognised degrees.
International student admission
Specific admission procedure for international applicants. Administrative support, accommodation and integration on all campuses.
How to apply

Admission pathways

Several routes allow you to join JUNIA, depending on your profile and level of study.

Parcoursup
To join the integrated preparatory engineering cycle in year 1, after school (or equivalent).
Parallel admissions
To join directly in year 1 of the engineering cycle (Bac+2). Open to holders of BTS, DUT/BUT, degree or preparatory class.
International student admission
Dedicated procedure for foreign applicants wishing to join JUNIA. Specific application file required.
Work-study
Application via an employer or directly with JUNIA. Requires a signed apprenticeship or professionalisation contract.
Admissions calendar
Parcoursup applications open in January. Parallel admissions run throughout spring. Check the website for exact dates.
