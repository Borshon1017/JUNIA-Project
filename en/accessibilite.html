Accessibility declaration

Accessibility

JUNIA is committed to making its digital services accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities.

Accessibility standard

This website is designed to comply with WCAG 2.2 level AA accessibility criteria. We have taken care to: use semantic HTML5 markup, provide sufficient colour contrast, make navigation keyboard-accessible, add ARIA labels to interactive elements, and use native system fonts for optimal readability.

Known limitations

Some content may not yet fully comply with all WCAG 2.2 AA criteria. We are working to continuously improve accessibility. If you encounter a barrier, please report it using the contact details below.

Report an issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, you can report it by contacting JUNIA via the contact page. Please describe the page concerned and the difficulty encountered. We commit to responding within 5 working days.

Disability mission

JUNIA has a dedicated disability mission to support students with disabilities throughout their studies. Specific academic arrangements, adapted facilities and dedicated support are available on all three campuses (Lille, Bordeaux, Châteauroux). Contact our disability mission team via the contact page.

Eco-design & accessibility

The eco-design approach of this website (native system fonts, no external images, minimal CSS) also contributes to accessibility by improving loading speed and readability, particularly for users with visual impairments or slow connections.
