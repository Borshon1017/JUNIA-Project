Admission routes

How to join JUNIA

Several pathways allow you to join JUNIA. Choose the one that matches your current situation.

Parcoursup
To join the integrated preparatory engineering cycle in year 1, after school leaving certificate (or equivalent). Applications open in January.
Parallel admissions
To join directly in year 1 of the engineering cycle (Bac+2). Open to holders of BTS, DUT/BUT, degree or preparatory class. Applications throughout spring.
International students
Dedicated procedure for foreign applicants. Online application, interview in French or English. JUNIA supports you throughout your visa and accommodation procedures.
Work-study
Apply with an employer or directly with JUNIA. Requires a signed apprenticeship or professionalisation contract. Bac+2 required for the engineering cycle.
Continuing education & RPL
Are you a working professional? JUNIA offers flexible programmes and Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) options. Contact the admissions team for more information.
Exchange programmes
Are you a student at a partner university? Join JUNIA for a semester or year under your inter-institutional exchange programme.

Tuition fees

Tuition fees & funding

Fees vary by programme, level and status. Several funding options are available.

Grande École Engineering Cycle
Tuition fees for the Grande École cycle (full-time) are detailed in our brochure. Scholarships and social grants are available for eligible students.
Work-study
In work-study, tuition fees are covered by the company. You also receive a salary throughout the programme. No personal cost to the student.
Scholarships & grants
CROUS scholarships, JUNIA merit scholarships, regional grants, international grants. Our admissions team will tell you which options you are eligible for.
