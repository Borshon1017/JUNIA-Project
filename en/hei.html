Identity & Positioning

HEI, the generalist engineer
committed to transitions

HEI — Hautes Études d'Ingénieur — is one of JUNIA's three Grande École programmes. Founded in 1885, it is one of the oldest engineering programmes in France. HEI trains generalist, versatile and committed engineers, capable of working on complex issues at the intersection of energy, buildings, industry and urban systems.

HEI's guiding thread: transitions. Energy transition, industrial transition, urban transition. Every HEI student is trained to understand these challenges in depth and provide technical, human and systemic responses.

Specialities & Fields

HEI specialisation areas

From year 3, HEI students choose their specialisation path from the major fields of transition engineering.

Energy & Smart Grids
Energy production and distribution systems, smart networks, energy storage, integration of renewables. Designing tomorrow's energy infrastructure.
Sustainable Building & Smart Building
Building energy performance, thermal renovation, building management systems (BMS), positive energy buildings (BEPOS), HQE and BREEAM certifications.
Industry 4.0 & Production Systems
Advanced automation, collaborative robotics, cobots, industrial IoT, predictive maintenance, digital twins, lean manufacturing applied to transitions.
City in Transition & Mobility
Smart city, urban network management, soft and electric mobility, territorial planning, sustainable infrastructure. Tomorrow's city as designed by engineers.
Industrial Engineering & Management
Managing complex industrial projects, responsible supply chain, organisational transition management, change management in business.
Environment & Sustainable Development
Life cycle assessment (LCA), carbon footprint, ISO 14001, industrial eco-design, industrial waste and resource management.
