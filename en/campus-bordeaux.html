The city

Studying in Bordeaux

Bordeaux is one of France's most attractive cities — exceptional quality of life, economic dynamism and proximity to the Atlantic and the Pyrenees.

Quality of life
Bordeaux is regularly ranked among the best French cities for quality of life. UNESCO heritage, a rich cultural scene, proximity to the Atlantic coast and the famous Bordeaux vineyards.
Job market
The Bordeaux metropolitan area has a diverse economic ecosystem: aeronautics, digital, health industries, agri-food and energy. An ideal environment for internships and work-study placements.
Student city
Bordeaux welcomes more than 100,000 students. A rich cultural, sports and social scene. A city that thrives on its youth, with a dynamic and engaged student community.
Health services
The Bordeaux campus provides health support for students, including medical services and well-being resources to ensure the best possible study conditions.
