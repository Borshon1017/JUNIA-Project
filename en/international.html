JUNIA students

Study abroad during your programme

JUNIA encourages and facilitates international mobility. Several options are available depending on the programme and desired duration.

Semester abroad
Spend a semester at one of JUNIA's 180+ partner universities. Courses taken abroad are integrated into your curriculum and recognised in your degree.
International double degrees
Obtain your JUNIA degree and that of a partner foreign university simultaneously. A double degree programme typically lasts 1 to 2 years abroad.
Erasmus+ programmes
JUNIA is an Erasmus+ member. Benefit from an Erasmus+ grant to fund your mobility in Europe. Additional grants may be available.
International placements
Complete your final or intermediate placement at a company abroad. JUNIA's Career Centre supports you in your search.
University partnerships
JUNIA maintains active partnerships with 180+ universities on every continent: Asia, North and South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania.
International experiences
International projects, inter-campus meetings, global hackathons. Beyond semesters, JUNIA multiplies opportunities to meet students from around the world.
International students

Are you an international student?

JUNIA welcomes students from around the world and offers dedicated support to ease your transition to studying in France.

International admission procedure
A specific admission procedure is available for international applicants. Online application, interview in French or English depending on the programme.
Exchange programmes
Exchange programmes allow international students to join JUNIA for a semester or full year under inter-institutional agreements.
Welcome & accommodation
JUNIA assists international students in finding accommodation at the Lille, Bordeaux and Châteauroux campuses. Student residences are available.
Administrative procedures
Visa, residence permit, student social security: JUNIA guides you through all administrative steps so you can study in France with peace of mind.
Multilingual campuses
Some courses are taught in English, particularly in programmes with a strong international component. The environment is open and multicultural.
International contact
JUNIA's international team answers all your questions about admissions, accommodation and integration.

International policy

International at the heart of JUNIA's strategy

International openness is not an optional module at JUNIA: it is a pillar of every JUNIA engineer's formation.

Partner campuses
JUNIA has a network of international partner campuses. These campuses welcome JUNIA students under equivalent academic conditions.
Active university partnerships
JUNIA's 180+ university partnerships are governed by active agreements, regularly reviewed to ensure the quality of the student experience.
English-language teaching
English-language courses are integrated into the curricula of all three programmes. Students are prepared to work in international professional environments.
