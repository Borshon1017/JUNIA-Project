Common foundation

What unites the 3 programmes

Beyond their specific areas, HEI, ISEN and ISA share the same vision and the same academic standards within JUNIA.

CTI degree for all three
All three programmes award a degree accredited by the Commission des Titres d'Ingénieur (CTI), recognised in France and internationally (Master's grade).
Shared preparatory cycle
The first two years of study form a shared preparatory cycle. HEI, ISEN and ISA students attend common foundational modules together.
Transitions as the guiding thread
Every programme integrates the challenges of energy, digital and environmental transitions. Training engineer-citizens is at the heart of JUNIA's pedagogy.
Shared industrial demonstrators
Students from all three programmes access JUNIA's 4 industrial demonstrators: Factory of the future, City in transition, Agriculture & food of tomorrow.
Shared international opportunities
180+ partner universities accessible to students from all three programmes. Erasmus+, double degrees and long stays available to everyone.
Unified JUNIA alumni network
42,000 alumni share the same network, regardless of original programme. The Career Centre supports all HEI, ISEN and ISA graduates.
Recognition

Networks & Labels

JUNIA is a member of recognised networks that guarantee the quality and international reach of its programmes.

CTI — Commission des Titres d'Ingénieur
All three HEI, ISEN and ISA programmes are CTI-accredited. The engineering degree is recognised at Master's level (Bac+5) across Europe.
CGE — Conférence des Grandes Écoles
JUNIA is a member of the Conférence des Grandes Écoles, a label that guarantees academic quality and professional integration outcomes.
Erasmus+ and international partnerships
JUNIA is an Erasmus+ member and partner to 180+ universities on 5 continents. Partner campuses welcome JUNIA students for exchange periods.
